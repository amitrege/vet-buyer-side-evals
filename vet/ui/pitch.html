The idea

Buyer request

After-hours calls for a chain of urgent-care clinics in Houston. Callers switch between Spanish and English mid-sentence, they're on cell phones in noisy rooms, and they read out prescriptions and callback numbers. I need the transcription layer that gets drug names and callback numbers right — I dial those numbers back.

Step one — what to test

Spoken by a Spanish-accented voice, over a noisy phone line.

Step three — the answer key

How do we know the right answer?

Because we wrote the sentence first — then had it spoken out loud.
